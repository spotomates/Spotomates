At a glance

Cozy Express is made by Spotomates LLC. This policy covers the game on iPhone and iPad, and its optional Friends service. Questions about privacy can go to spots@spotomates.com.

You do not need a Friends account to play solo. Your house, railway, saved layouts, local profile names and private labels for friends’ houses are stored on your device. We do not upload these local saves as a cloud backup. When Friends is on, a look-only copy of your main house is shared with accepted friends, as described below; it is not a backup.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or include advertising trackers in the game. Friends does not ask for an email address, real name, birthday, location, access to your device’s address book, camera or microphone access.

When you turn on Friends

Friends needs some information to connect the right players and deliver their gifts. A random account identifier still identifies an account, even though it is not your real name.

  • Your account: a random identifier, friend code, name picked from the game’s word lists, and protected sign-in and recovery credentials. The service stores hashes of secret credentials.
  • Your connections: friend requests, accepted friends and blocked players.
  • Your shared house: your main house, inside and out. That is the exterior with its roof lights and fences, and a look-only copy of the inside: the house as your game saves it, its furniture and fireplace, its lighting, snow, fire, train and tree lights, and your tree with the painted ornaments hanging on it. Gifts friends sent you show too: an opened ornament hanging on your tree shows its painting, and wrapped gifts on the tree, under it or in the stockings show as wrapped boxes. Names, gift tags, messages and anything typed are not included. Turning on Friends shares the house straight away, and Stop sharing hides it again. Friends does not upload your other houses, local profiles or private labels.
  • Visits and the visitors center: when you go inside a friend’s shared house, the game sends updates about your visit. We keep one summary for each house owner and visitor: their account identifiers, visit count, first and most recent visit times, and the latest presence and departure times reported by the game. We also keep the latest 60 visits to each house: who came in, when they arrived and when they left. The house owner can see which friends visited, how often, when each visit began, how long it lasted and whether they appear to be inside now. Standing at the front door does not count.
  • Counts friends see: the number of houses you have built, which your game sends, and counts the service makes from its gift and visit records: gifts you have given and received, friends’ houses you have been inside, and how many visits your house has had, from how many friends. Friends get the numbers only, not who they involve.
  • What you send: ornament paintings and their names, painted wreaths and cookie icing sent as gifts, and the preset message you choose. If a friend hangs an ornament you sent on their tree, their other accepted friends can see its painting when they visit, without your name.
  • Delivery and collections: gift identifiers, sending, arrival and opening times, and saved ornament artwork needed for delivery or account recovery. The sender sees whether each gift is on its way, under the friend’s tree or opened, and when.
  • Safety: a report’s chosen reason and a copy of the reported gift or shared house, plus any resulting moderation action.
  • Service protection: the date the account was last used, and small records used to enforce limits and prevent duplicate gifts.

Friends does not receive Full Game purchase records; see Buying the Full Game.

Accepted friends can see your picked name and visit your shared house, including inside it. Gifts arrive wrapped; their contents are shown to the recipient when opened. There is no public profile feed or public gallery for Friends. A name you privately give a friend’s house stays on your own device.

Apple age checks and information for parents

Friends is for players aged 13 and over. On a supported iPhone or iPad, Apple’s Declared Age Range service must return a range that establishes eligibility before Friends connects. An under-13 result, declined sharing, an unavailable check or an unknown result leaves Friends off. Solo play stays available.

We do not ask you to type a birthday or age. Apple’s returned range is used in memory on the device; it is not saved by the game or sent to our Friends service. Buying anything does not replace the age check.

The Apple signal relates to the Apple Account on the device, which may not be the person currently playing on a shared device. Each local profile must pass its own check. If you believe an under-13 player is using Friends, contact spots@spotomates.com. We will investigate and take appropriate action. A declined or failed check alone does not delete an existing account.

Buying the Full Game

Apple handles payment for the Full Game. We never receive your name, Apple Account details, payment card or address.

The game checks your purchase with Apple on your device. So that the Full Game stays open when you are offline, it saves a small record of Apple’s confirmation on your device: Apple’s transaction numbers and dates, any refund date, and whether the purchase came through Family Sharing. That record stays on your device.

The game does not send purchase records to our Friends service, and the service does not contact Apple about your purchase. The extra friend slots that come with the Full Game are unlocked by the game on your device. Solo play does not use our service, before or after you buy.

If we later start checking purchases through our service, we will update this policy and explain the change before it applies.

In-game screenshots

The game’s camera button takes a screenshot of the game: your village, room or train as the game draws it. It never uses your device’s camera, microphone or screen recording.

We do not collect or store your screenshots. They are never sent to our servers, logs or analytics, and friends cannot see them. A screenshot you don’t save is thrown away when you close its preview.

When you save one, it goes into the Photos app on your device. The game only asks permission to add it there; it cannot see anything else in your Photos app.

If you share a screenshot, or iCloud syncs your Photos app, the app or service you chose handles that copy.

How long information is kept

We keep information for account access, gift delivery, saved collections and safety—not simply because storage is available. The service runs daily cleanup, so timed deletion happens on the cleanup pass after a record reaches its limit.

Your choices and account deletion

You can stop sharing your house, remove or block a friend, discard gifts, and remove ornaments from your server collection. Removing or blocking a friend deletes visit summaries and visit times between your accounts in both directions. Stopping house sharing keeps previous visit information. To leave Friends completely, open Friends → Delete my online data and follow the confirmations. When access to Friends is unavailable, the account and safety controls remain available.

Deleting the account removes its credentials, connections, blocks, shared house, visit summaries and visit times as both host and visitor, sent and received delivery records, owned collections and server copies of its artwork. It does not affect a Full Game purchase, which stays with your Apple Account. Limited evidence may remain while a safety report is investigated or when preservation is required.

Deletion also clears this device’s account-specific Friends inbox and received-gift journal. Your own house and decorations stay on your device. Separate saved wreath-shelf and cookie-tin entries are not a complete account backup and can be removed locally. Already-opened gifts on other players’ devices may remain.

You can ask to access, correct or delete information by emailing spots@spotomates.com. We may need to verify account ownership before changing or disclosing account information. A public friend code alone does not authorize account deletion.

Service providers, connections and support

Render hosts the Friends service and database. Service providers process information to operate and protect the service; they are not given permission to use Friends content for advertising. Apple handles its age-range service, the App Store and any Apple payment processing under Apple’s own policies.

A network request necessarily exposes connection information such as an IP address to the service and hosting providers. Our application keeps IP addresses and request counters in service memory to enforce abuse limits. It does not put addresses, account names, gift contents or request bodies in its application logs. Hosting providers may maintain their own operational logs.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue and handle related account or safety requests. Support correspondence is separate from the Friends database and is kept while needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

These website pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and set no cookies themselves. The website host may process connection information to serve and protect the pages. Opening an external link or sending email uses the destination service’s own privacy practices.
