At a glance

Cozy Express is made by Spotomates LLC. This policy covers the iPad game and its optional Friends service. Questions about privacy can go to spots@spotomates.com.

You do not need a Friends account to play solo. Your house, railway, saved layouts, local profile names and private labels for friends’ houses are stored on your iPad. We do not upload these local saves as a cloud backup. When Friends is on, a look-only copy of your main house is shared with accepted friends, as described below; it is not a backup.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or include advertising trackers in the game. Friends does not ask for an email address, real name, birthday, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

When you turn on Friends

Friends needs some information to connect the right players and deliver their gifts. A random account identifier still identifies an account, even though it is not your real name.

  • Your account: a random identifier, friend code, name picked from the game’s word lists, and protected sign-in and recovery credentials. The service stores hashes of secret credentials.
  • Your connections: friend requests, accepted friends and blocked players.
  • Your shared house: your main house, inside and out. That is the exterior with its roof lights and fences, and a look-only copy of the inside: the house as your game saves it, its furniture and fireplace, its lighting, snow, fire, train and tree lights, and your tree with the painted ornaments hanging on it. Gifts friends sent you show too: an opened ornament hanging on your tree shows its painting, and wrapped gifts on the tree, under it or in the stockings show as wrapped boxes. Names, gift tags, messages and anything typed are not included. Turning on Friends shares the house straight away, and Stop sharing hides it again. Friends does not upload your other houses, local profiles or private labels.
  • What you send: ornament paintings and their names, painted wreaths and cookie icing sent as gifts, and the preset message you choose. If a friend hangs an ornament you sent on their tree, their other accepted friends can see its painting when they visit, without your name.
  • Delivery and collections: gift identifiers, sending/opening times, and saved ornament artwork needed for delivery or account recovery.
  • Safety: a report’s chosen reason and a copy of the reported gift or shared house, plus any resulting moderation action.
  • Service protection: the date the account was last used, and small records used to enforce limits and prevent duplicate gifts.

Accepted friends can see your picked name and visit your shared house, including inside it. Gifts arrive wrapped; their contents are shown to the recipient when opened. There is no public profile feed or public gallery for iPad Friends. A name you privately give a friend’s house stays on your own iPad.

Apple age checks and information for parents

Friends is for players aged 13 and over. On a supported iPad, Apple’s Declared Age Range service must return a range that establishes eligibility before Friends connects. An under-13 result, declined sharing, an unavailable check or an unknown result leaves Friends off. Solo play stays available.

We do not ask you to type a birthday or age. Apple’s returned range is used in memory on the iPad; it is not saved by the game or sent to our Friends service. Buying anything does not replace the age check.

The Apple signal relates to the Apple Account on the device, which may not be the person currently playing on a shared iPad. Each local profile must pass its own check. If you believe an under-13 player is using Friends, contact spots@spotomates.com. We will investigate and take appropriate action. A declined or failed check alone does not delete an existing account.

Photos you take in the game

The photo feature creates a picture of the game’s rendered scene on your device. It does not use the camera, microphone or screen-recording service.

A picture stays local until you choose to save or share it. Saving to Photos requests permission to add that picture, not to read your photo library. We do not send these pictures to Cozy Express servers or attach image content to analytics or logs.

A sharing destination you select, or your own Photos sync settings, may transfer an exported image off the iPad. Those destinations handle the exported copy under their own settings and policies.

How long information is kept

We keep information for account access, gift delivery, saved collections and safety—not simply because storage is available. The service runs daily cleanup, so timed deletion happens on the cleanup pass after a record reaches its limit.

Copies already saved locally are separate from server retention. An opened gift may remain on a recipient’s iPad after ordinary server expiry or another player’s deletion. Moderation can remove reported content when the iPad reconnects.

Protected service backups may retain deleted records until those backups expire. Contact us if you need information about a deletion request or backup retention.

Your choices and account deletion

You can stop sharing your house, remove or block a friend, discard gifts, and remove ornaments from your server collection. To leave Friends completely, open Friends → Delete my online data and follow the confirmations. When access to Friends is unavailable, the account and safety controls remain available.

Deleting the account removes its credentials, connections, blocks, shared house, sent and received delivery records, owned collections, and server copies of its artwork. Limited evidence may remain while a safety report is investigated or when preservation is required.

Deletion also clears this iPad’s account-specific Friends inbox and received-gift journal. Your own house and decorations stay on your iPad. Separate saved wreath-shelf and cookie-tin entries are not a complete account backup and can be removed locally. Already-opened gifts on other players’ iPads may remain.

You can ask to access, correct or delete information by emailing spots@spotomates.com. We may need to verify account ownership before changing or disclosing account information. A public friend code alone does not authorize account deletion.

Service providers, connections and support

Render hosts the Friends service and database. Service providers process information to operate and protect the service; they are not given permission to use Friends content for advertising. Apple handles its age-range service, the App Store and any Apple payment processing under Apple’s own policies.

A network request necessarily exposes connection information such as an IP address to the service and hosting providers. Our application uses addresses for abuse limits and does not put addresses, account names, gift contents or request bodies in its application logs. Hosting providers may maintain their own operational logs.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue and handle related account or safety requests. Support correspondence is separate from the Friends database and is kept while needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

These website pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and set no cookies themselves. The website host may process connection information to serve and protect the pages. Opening an external link or sending email uses the destination service’s own privacy practices.
