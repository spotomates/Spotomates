Friends rules

Keep shared houses and little gifts welcoming, kind and free of personal details.

Updated November 3, 2026 · Spotomates LLC

01Friends is for players 13 and over

Friends requires a supported Apple age-range check that establishes eligibility. Do not bypass the check or use someone else’s profile to avoid it. Buying the full game does not grant age eligibility.

If Friends is unavailable, you can still enjoy your local game. An Apple Account on a shared iPad does not necessarily identify the person playing.

02Keep personal details out of shared creations

Do not put real names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, schools, locations, account handles or other private information into anything you share.

This includes ornament paintings, cookie icing, roof-light drawings and fences. Do not share someone else’s personal details or try to move another player into private contact through a drawing.

Use a picked Friends name. A private label you give a friend’s house stays on your iPad. Keep your recovery code private; it is not a friend code.

03Be kind and keep content appropriate

Bullying, harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, violent content, exploitation, scams and illegal material are not allowed. Do not use drawings or repeated gifts to insult, pressure or frighten someone.

Unkind or abusive content and players are not tolerated. Do not evade a block, suspension or content removal by changing accounts or resending the same material.

04Share only work you have permission to share

Only send creations you made or are allowed to share. Respect other people’s work.

By using Friends, you give Spotomates LLC permission to store and copy the content you choose to share, show shared exteriors to accepted friends, deliver gifts to recipients, and review reported material for safety. We use that permission to operate Friends and handle safety concerns, with retention and deletion described in the privacy policy.

You can stop sharing a house or delete your account. Recipients may still keep gifts they already opened locally; deletion does not recall every copy on another person’s iPad.

05Report concerns and use blocking

Use Report gift or Report house and select the reason that fits. Use Block to stop visits and gifts from a player. Make reports honestly and do not use them to harass someone.

These controls and account deletion remain available without a purchase, including when Friends access is denied or an account is suspended. You do not need to open a wrapped gift to report it.

If you cannot use a report control, or want to discuss a safety concern, email spots@spotomates.com. Do not send suspected illegal material by email.

06We may remove content or suspend access

We review reports and may remove a gift, stop a house being shared or suspend a Friends account. Removed content may be cleared from saved Friends copies when an iPad next connects. We may preserve limited report evidence when required.

A Friends suspension does not remove access to solo play or erase your own house. You can still contact support, report, block and delete the account. If you believe an action was a mistake, ask us to review it.

Agreeing to the rules

The game asks you to agree before enabling Friends, including on an existing account that has not accepted the current rules. Choosing to keep playing solo leaves Friends off. The game remembers agreement locally for the relevant profile and account.

If the rules materially change, we will explain the changes and ask for agreement again before Friends resumes.
