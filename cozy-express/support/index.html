Getting into Friends

Why does Friends ask to check with Apple?

Friends is optional and requires an Apple age range that establishes 13+. Tap Check with Apple and choose whether to share your range. The game does not ask for a typed birthday or accept a manual age declaration.

If the range is under 13, sharing is declined, or the check cannot confirm eligibility, keep playing solo. Report, block, account deletion and these support links remain available for an existing account.

Which devices can use Friends?

The Apple age-range check needs iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later and a supported, eligible Apple response. On an older or unsupported device, solo play remains available. An age pass does not override Apple communication restrictions.

Why do I need to check again?

Friends checks eligibility again when you return to the app or change your local profile or account. Tap Check with Apple to reconnect. Creating or recovering an account may also need another check for that account. The game does not automatically repeat Apple’s sharing prompt.

To review Apple’s preference, open Settings, choose your name, then Personal Information and Age Range for Apps. Return to the game and deliberately retry.

How do I add a friend or send a gift?

After the age check and Friends rules agreement, choose a name from the word lists and turn on Friends. Exchange friend codes outside the game with someone you know. One player enters the other’s code, and the other accepts.

Your house is shared with accepted friends as soon as Friends is on, and they can look inside. Stop sharing hides it, and Show my house to friends shares it again. Visit a shared house in the Neighborhood and tap Go inside to look around. To send a gift, tap Leave a gift, choose an ornament, wreath or cookie and a preset message, then wrap it. The recipient can be offline.

Keep personal information out of ornament paintings and names, wreaths, icing, roofs and fences. If a friend hangs an ornament you sent on their tree, their other friends can see its painting when they visit. See the Friends rules.

Friends will not connect, or the account is suspended

Check your internet connection and use the latest game version. An unavailable service does not erase your house or local creations. If the game says the account is suspended, use the account/safety controls or email us; repeating the age check does not lift a suspension.

Recovery and moving to another device

Keep the recovery code shown in Friends somewhere private. Choose the recovery option on the new iPhone or iPad and enter it after the age check and rules agreement. Recovery replaces the old credentials, so the previous device is signed out of that account.

Friends recovery is not a full game backup. It can restore the account, connections, retained deliveries and saved ornament collection. It does not restore the complete local house, railway, placements or other local saves.

Gift deliveries expire 13 months after sending. Wreaths and cookies cannot be recovered from the service once their delivery expires. An account deleted by you or removed after 13 months without use cannot be recovered with its old code.

If you lose the code, email us for help. A friend code alone does not prove account ownership, and we cannot promise account recovery without suitable verification.

Report a concern or block a player

  • A gift: open its report control and choose Unkind picture, Personal details or Something else. The safety screen also lets you report a saved gift without reopening Friends.
  • A house: choose Report house from the friend row or while at their door.
  • A player: choose Block and confirm. Blocking stops future visits and gifts between you.

If your friends list has not loaded yet, choose Report or block a player on the Friends tab to report or block someone from your account’s list.

Reporting, blocking and account deletion are free and stay available when an age check is pending or denied, or the account is suspended. A wrapped gift can be reported without opening it. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content that breaks the Friends rules.

For an urgent concern, suspected under-13 participation or a problem using the report button, email spots@spotomates.com. Include the relevant friend code if you have it and a brief explanation. Do not forward suspected illegal material.

Delete your Friends account

  1. Open Friends.
  2. Find Delete my online data. If Friends is unavailable, use the Account section on the access screen.
  3. Read and confirm the deletion prompts.

This deletes the online account, connections, shared house and server gift records, and clears this device’s account-specific inbox and received-gift journal. It does not erase your own house or decorations. Separate wreath-shelf and cookie-tin items may remain locally; remove them there if you wish.

Already-opened gifts can remain on other recipients’ devices. Limited safety evidence or protected backup copies may remain as described in the privacy policy. For an account deletion request when you cannot use the game, email support; we will need to verify the request.

Saving and sharing screenshots

Tap the camera button to take a screenshot of the game. Check the preview, then save it to the Photos app, share it, or discard it. Taking a screenshot never uploads or publishes it.

If the game can’t save to the Photos app, allow it in the Settings app, or share the screenshot somewhere else. The game only asks to add screenshots, not to read your Photos app. Once you save or share a screenshot, that app or service handles the copy.

Purchases and the full game

Cozy Express offers a playable free game and one optional Full Game purchase on iPhone and iPad. There is no subscription, advertising, currency or countdown. Apple shows the price in your local currency before you confirm.

The Full Game unlocks all house styles, Cozy Cabin mode, furniture designs and finishes, roof drawing and lighting choices, wreath ingredients, train designs, additional saves and profiles, more space for your own ornament designs, and additional friends within the service limits. Normal device, placement and storage limits still apply.

Tiles and Free Build, comfort settings, and visits and gifts with an accepted friend remain part of the free game. A purchase never replaces the Friends age check. Reporting, blocking, support and account deletion are always free.

Restoring your purchase

On a new or reinstalled iPhone or iPad, sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Tap anything with a padlock to open the Full Game panel, then choose Restore purchase. If the unlock does not return, check your connection, restart the app and email us for help.

Restoring the purchase and recovering Friends are separate. Use your Friends recovery code to recover that account. Neither action restores a complete local house or railway save. Deleting a Friends account does not delete your Apple purchase.

Apple’s help with restoring purchases

Family Sharing

The Full Game purchase supports Apple Family Sharing. Eligible family members can use the unlock through their own Apple Accounts with purchase sharing enabled. If it does not appear, check the family’s purchase-sharing settings and use Restore purchase.

Family Sharing does not share a Friends account or override the age check. Each player still uses their own local profile and follows the Friends rules.

Refunds and changes to access

For a purchase billed by Apple, request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service. Apple handles eligibility and the refund decision. Read Apple’s refund instructions.

If paid access ends, premium creations and extra capacity are locked. Your original saved work is preserved and becomes usable again when valid paid access returns. Free play and eligible received gifts remain available. Your solo game does not depend on Friends. Friends is an online service that may change or end, and the purchase does not guarantee its continued availability.
